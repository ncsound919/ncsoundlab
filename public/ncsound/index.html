The studio

How this is built, and why it looks the way it does.

Local-first by default

NC Sound Lab stores kits, projects and takes in your browser or app sandbox. There is no account required to use it and no upload step to lose a session to.

Real DSP, not samples

Drums, 808s, leads and pads are synthesized by a ZDF ladder filter, hard-sync, wavefolding and granular stages. That is why a kit can be tuned to any root.

Sessions move between tools

A field take becomes a Phone Beat project, which opens in NC Studio, which exports AAF into Pro Tools. One .ncbeat package carries it.

Everything has a paper trail

The research side runs the same way the audio side does: deterministic, hash-verified, and reproducible after the fact.
